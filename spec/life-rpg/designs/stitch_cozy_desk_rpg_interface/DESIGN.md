## Brand & Style
The design system manifests an intimate, tactile, and heartwarming world reminiscent of late-night lofi study sessions, journal crafting, and quiet seaside towns. It channels the grounded serenity of *Coffee Talk*, the pastoral wonder of *Animal Crossing*, and the tender emotional depth of *Spiritfarer*.

### Design Movements & Philosophy
- **Tactile Illustrative Skeuomorphism:** Interfaces feel hand-crafted from heavy watercolor cardstock, worn leather book-cloth, stamped wax seals, and polished cedar tabletop surfaces. Interactive components possess physical dimensionality—thick beveled bottoms that depress on interaction, subtly debossed panels, and warm inner paper-grain glows.
- **Warm Whimsical Restraint:** The visual language avoids cold techno-minimalism while rejecting chaotic clutter. Every panel reads like a page in an explorer's pocket field journal or an apothecary's ledger.
- **Glow & Atmosphere:** Ambient lighting mimics golden lamplight, hearthside embers, and twilight window reflections rather than sterile digital dropshadows.

## Colors
The palette balances sun-drenched herbal tones with deeply grounded library mahogany and comforting tea-stained paper.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#D97724` Honey Amber):** The lifeblood of the interface; applied to primary CTAs, active selection indicators, star rewards, and high-priority notices. Reminiscent of wild clover honey and warm brass lanterns.
- **Secondary (`#58785E` Soft Sage):** Evokes wild mint, mossy stones, and quiet greenhouse gardens. Used for secondary navigation, completed tasks, inventory categorization, and positive state badges.
- **Tertiary (`#C85A3D` Terracotta Hearth):** A baked clay accent used for alerts, heart ratings, craftable items, and warm emotional triggers.
- **Neutral Base (`#2B1E1A` Deep Mahogany):** Replaces pure black entirely. A deeply saturated roasted-chestnut ink that provides strong contrast while retaining organic warmth.
- **Surface Canvas (`#F9F4E8` Parchment Cream):** The standard app surface, modeled after fibrous, unbleached cotton paper.
- **Atmospheric Accent (`#242746` Twilight Indigo):** Reserved for nocturnal overlays, dialogue box backdrops during night cycles, and high-rarity relic borders.
- **Illumination (`#FFE89E` Golden Lamplight):** Used for focal highlights, inner glow rims, and interactive hover auras.

## Typography
Typography bridges the playfulness of animated game HUDs with the enduring legibility of cozy storybook narratives.

### Roles and Styling
- **Display & Headlines (`Bricolage Grotesque`):** Delivers chunky, exuberant personality with friendly ink traps and expressive terminal flares. Always styled with high weights (`700` and `800`) to anchor dialogue banners, quest titles, and inventory category dividers.
- **Reading & Narrative (`Quicksand`):** Soft, rounded, and welcoming. Provides long-form comfort for dialogue scripts, item lore, recipe guides, and social logbooks without visual fatigue. Medium weights (`500`-`600`) ensure rich contrast against parchment backgrounds.
- **HUD Labels & Badges (`Epilogue`):** Sturdy, structured, and legible at small sizes. Used for inventory quantity badges, hotkey prompts, timestamps, resource counters, and button text.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to a structured yet comfortable modular grid designed around diegetic paper cards, notebook tabs, and wooden shelves.

### Grid & Composition
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout centered within a max canvas width of 1280px. Margins stay generous (`2rem` / `32px`) to leave visible framing of warm wooden background textures or serene dusk skyboxes.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout. Split-pane layouts (such as inventory grid alongside item inspection details) stack or collapse into swipeable parchment tabs.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with compact margins (`1rem` / `16px`) and tight gutters (`0.75rem` / `12px`). Modals and dialogues dock to the bottom viewport edge as "dialogue trays."

### Spacing Rhythm
Spacing relies on multiples of 6px and 12px translated into rem units, avoiding harsh, hyper-condensed layouts. Negative space is intentional and feels airy, inviting the user to linger rather than rush through workflows.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation abandons cold, fuzzy Material blurs in favor of physical paper stacking, wooden bevels, and warm hearth glows.

### Elevation Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Tabletop Base):** Deep cedar or aged walnut woodgrain background surface, deep-toned and grounded (`#201613`).
2. **Level 1 (Parchment Board):** Thick natural cream cardstock (`#F9F4E8`) with a crisp, 2px solid mahogany stroke (`#2B1E1A`), complemented by a hard directional drop-shadow: `0 4px 0 #2B1E1A`.
3. **Level 2 (Raised Tokens & Active Cards):** Floating menu strips, item slots, and floating tooltips. Shadow: `0 6px 0 #2B1E1A` paired with a soft warm amber ambient haze: `0 8px 16px rgba(217, 119, 36, 0.15)`.
4. **Level 3 (Tactile Pressables):** Buttons and physical toggles feature an artificial bottom bevel created by a distinct color offset (4px lower edge in darker wood or shaded amber) that collapses down to `0px` with a 2px translate-y on `:active`.
5. **Overlays & Night Windows:** High-elevation modals feature a semi-translucent scrim tinted in Twilight Indigo (`#242746` at 70% opacity) layered with a soft golden inner rim glow: `inset 0 0 12px rgba(255, 232, 158, 0.35)`.

## Shapes
Shapes celebrate rounded corner geometry (`0.5rem` / 8px for standard items, `1rem` / 16px for containers, `1.5rem` / 24px for major frames), evocative of hand-whittled wooden tokens, river pebbles, and curved postage stamps.

### Detailing Rules
- **Crisp Outer Inking:** Surfaces have an explicit 2px solid border (`#2B1E1A`), grounding them visually like storybook line art.
- **Subtle Organic Insets:** Nested containers (such as inventory item wells) use an inverted border or debossed inset shadow (`inset 0 2px 4px rgba(43, 30, 26, 0.12)`) paired with rounded inner geometry.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Honey Plaque"):** Background is Honey Amber (`#D97724`), text is Parchment Cream (`#F9F4E8`), set in `label-lg`. Framed with a 2px `#2B1E1A` border and an extruded bottom edge (`box-shadow: 0 4px 0 #9E4D0E`). On click/press, translates down 4px with zero shadow to simulate a physical chunky wooden switch.
- **Secondary ("Sage Field"):** Soft Sage green background (`#58785E`), 2px border, and a darker moss base shadow (`box-shadow: 0 4px 0 #38503C`).
- **Ghost/Tertiary ("Parchment Ribbon"):** Transparent background with `#2B1E1A` text, underlined with a playful wavy dash or a warm honey dot hover state.

### Cards & Dialogue Panels
- Framed in unbleached parchment cream with a 2px `#2B1E1A` border and 16px rounded corners.
- Top-left badges mimic pinned wax seals, postage stamps, or hand-tied luggage tags.
- Dialogue boxes feature a carved character portrait cutout on the left, an amber speaker-name plaque overlapping the top frame, and a blinking acorn/leaf prompt at the bottom right indicating text progression.

### Inventory Slots & Chips
- **Item Slots:** Square or slightly rounded tiles with an inset inner shadow (`#E8DCBE`). An active or hovered slot lights up with an inner 2px Golden Lamplight glow (`#FFE89E`) and a micro-bounce translation (`translateY(-2px)`).
- **Filter Chips:** Styled like fabric ribbon bookmarks or rounded seed packets. Active chips turn Soft Sage with deep forest text; inactive chips remain parchment paper with 1.5px dotted borders.

### Inputs & Sliders
- **Text Fields:** Styled as indented ledger spaces on warm cardstock. Background is off-white linen (`#F3EBD8`), border is 2px `#2B1E1A`, focus ring is a diffused 3px halo of warm amber (`rgba(217, 119, 36, 0.4)`).
- **Sliders & Gauges:** Track is drawn as a carved wooden groove (`#E1D3B5`). The handle is a round, chunky brass knob or sculpted wooden bead (`#D97724` with golden rim highlight) that subtly swells on drag.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkboxes:** Stylized as wooden stamp tiles. Unchecked is an empty hollow bordered in `#2B1E1A`. Checked reveals a stamped, terracotta-colored leaf, sprout, or star emblem.
- **Radios:** Rounded river-pebble shape containing an embossed honey-drop center when selected.

### Game-Specific HUD Elements
- **Resource Counters:** Pill containers framed in leather-brown with a glowing circular icon medallion docked seamlessly onto the left edge.
- **Friendship / Warmth Gauges:** Tiered heart or steaming teacup icons that fill progressively with terracotta and golden-honey liquids, emitting soft sparkling amber particle animations when maxed.
